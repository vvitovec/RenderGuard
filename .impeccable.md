## Design Context

### Users
Accounts-payable reviewers at European SMEs using an AI assistant to prepare repeat-supplier EUR/SEPA payments. Their task is to release an exact, evidence-supported payment or hold it for independent supplier verification. Hackathon judges also inspect controls and test unexpected interactions. Viktor delegated workflow and product decisions to this project.

### Brand Personality
Precise, accountable, composed. A payment release desk, with the clarity of a ledger and a marked-up paper invoice.

### Aesthetic Direction
Daytime finance desk: warm paper surfaces, deep forest ink, restrained vermilion for discrepancies. Literata headings and Public Sans body text, tabular financial numerals. Editorial spacing, fine rules, a compact document queue and asymmetric invoice/evidence/release workbench. No neon security dashboard, generic metric-card grid, fake savings or chatbot homepage.

### Design Principles
- Put the exact recipient, amount and source evidence before any abstract security metric.
- Visually separate proposed, approved and executed actions.
- Explain a hold with a concrete next action using independently stored supplier contact details.
- Keep specialist controls discoverable in a separate control-room view.
- Make every visible action operational and every statistic derived from persisted events.
